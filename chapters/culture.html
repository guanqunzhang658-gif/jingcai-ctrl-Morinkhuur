Culture & Stories

拨动岁月的琴弦，解码马头琴的文化基因。从草原神话的悠远回响，到跨界融合的焕新吟唱。

Plucking the strings of time, decoding the cultural genes of the Morin Khuur. From the distant echoes of grassland myths to the renewed singing of cross-boundary integration.

该图谱围绕马头琴文化中的器物、声音、工艺、纹样、历史与传承等内容展开，尝试梳理不同文化要素之间的关联关系。马头琴并非单一的乐器形态，其文化意义往往同时涉及草原游牧生活、马文化崇拜、民间叙事、音乐表达与非遗传承等多个层面。相关研究中常将这类内容视为传统器乐文化的重要组成部分，并认为其价值不仅体现在演奏功能上，也体现在民族记忆、审美符号和文化传播之中。图中各节点用于呈现马头琴文化内容的聚合关系，连线则表示不同要素之间可能存在的主题联系。

This atlas explores the cultural genes of the Morin Khuur, encompassing instruments, sound, craftsmanship, patterns, history, and heritage, attempting to map the relationships between different cultural elements. The Morin Khuur is not merely a single instrument; its cultural significance spans grassland nomadic life, horse culture worship, folk narratives, musical expression, and intangible cultural heritage. Research often regards these elements as vital components of traditional instrumental culture, valuing them not just for performance but as symbols of ethnic memory, aesthetics, and cultural transmission. The nodes in this diagram present the aggregation of Morin Khuur cultural content, while the connecting lines represent thematic associations between these elements.
